

# 🌩️ GCP Free Tier Account Kaise Banaye (Step-by-Step)

---

## 🔵 Step 1: Official Website Open Karo

Go to:

👉 [https://cloud.google.com](https://cloud.google.com)

Top right me **"Get started for free"** button milega.

Uspe click karo.

---

## 🔵 Step 2: Google Account Se Login

Agar Gmail account hai → direct login karo
Nahi hai → pehle Gmail bana lo

---

## 🔵 Step 3: Country Select Karo

Country choose karo: **India**
Terms accept karo

---

## 🔵 Step 4: Payment Details Dena Zaruri Hai ⚠️

Yaha sab log confuse hote hain.

GCP free tier ke liye:

* Debit/Credit card mandatory hai
* ₹2 ya ₹1 verification charge lagta hai (temporary hold)
* Ye charge refund ho jata hai

⚠️ Jab tak manually upgrade nahi karte, Google automatically charge nahi karta (lekin limits cross na karo).

---

## 🔵 Step 5: Free Trial Details Samjho

GCP deta hai:

### ✅ $300 Free Credits (90 days ke liye)

Aur kuch services hamesha free rehti hain (Always Free Tier)

---

# 🟢 GCP Always Free Tier (Important for DevOps)

Ye services hamesha free milti hain:

✔ 1 f1-micro VM instance (specific region me)
✔ 30GB HDD storage
✔ 5GB snapshot
✔ 1GB outbound data/month

---

# 🔵 Step 6: Billing Account Create Hoga

GCP automatically billing account create karega.

Dashboard open ho jayega.

---

# 🔵 Step 7: Project Create Karo

Top bar me:

"Select a Project" → "New Project"

Project name de do.

Example:
devops-practice
k8s-lab
terraform-lab

---

# 🔵 Step 8: Free Tier VM Kaise Banaye

1. Compute Engine → VM Instances
2. Enable API
3. Create Instance
4. Machine type choose karo:
   👉 e2-micro (Free tier eligible)
5. Region choose karo:
   👉 us-central1 (recommended for free tier)
6. Boot disk:
   Ubuntu / Debian

Create pe click karo.

---

# ⚠️ Important Free Tier Rules

Agar:

* VM e2-micro nahi li
* Region wrong li
* Extra disk zyada li

To charges lag sakte hain.

Isliye carefully select karo.

---

# 🔥 Safety Tips (Very Important)

1. Billing → Budget Alert set karo
2. $10 ka budget alert bana do
3. Idle VM band kar diya karo
4. Agar use nahi kar rahe → delete kar do

---

# 💰 Free Tier Duration

* $300 credit = 90 days
* Always free services = lifetime (limit ke andar)
